FOUNDER

Having grown up immersed in Japanese culture, I know this country's depth better than most. Japan holds the world's second-largest pool of household financial assets, yet much of its potential remains untapped. I founded LYTA CAPITAL to unleash that potential at the frontier of the world.

Five years in Silicon Valley showed me how faint Japan's presence has become at the technology frontier. Today I run several investment vehicles, including U.S. startup investments and an AI-native private equity roll-up platform. Alongside them, I lead cross-border advisory work connecting Silicon Valley's capital and technology with Japan's businesses and cultural assets, and an invitation-only program that brings the world's leaders into the centers of Japan's culture and industry.
